Privacy policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

Kuroot is a card game for playing Tarneeb, Syrian 41, Lebanese 400 and Spades with friends and family. This page says what the game keeps about you, why it keeps it, and how you can remove it. It is written to be read.

In short: you can play without giving a name, an email address or a phone number. The game has no advertising, no tracking and no analytics. It keeps what it needs to run your games and remember your progress, and you can delete all of it from inside the game at any time.

Who we are

Kuroot is published by Alex Toumayan. For anything about this policy or your information, write to kuroot.support@gmail.com.

What the game keeps

Your player

The first time you open Kuroot it makes a guest player for you: a random identifier, with no name or email needed. With it we keep what makes the game work: the display name you choose, your level, your gold coins, gold bars and trophies, the things you own and the ones you have chosen to use (decks, card backs, portraits, frames, things to throw), your daily gift and the days in a row you have collected it, your games and wins, and your friend code.

If you sign in with Apple

Signing in is optional. It ties your player to your Apple account so that your progress follows you to a new phone. When you do, Apple gives us an identifier for your Apple account, the email address Apple shares with us (your own, or a private relay address if you choose "Hide My Email"), and your first name if you allow it. We use these only to recognise you when you sign in again. We never see your Apple password.

Friends and messages

If you add friends, we keep who your friends are, the invitations between you, and the messages you send each other, so that they can be delivered and shown in your conversation. Messages pass through a word filter. If a player or a message is reported, we keep the report and the message so that we can act on it.

Your games

While you play at an online table, our server keeps the table: who sat where, the cards and the moves. That is how the game is refereed fairly. The other players at your table see your display name, your picture, your level and the look you have chosen. The standings of the weekly and monthly events show players' display names and points to everyone.

Your clock, not your location

The game tells us the difference between your phone's clock and universal time, so that your daily gift turns over at your own midnight. It does not tell us where you are, and the game never asks for your location.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your card or payment details. We receive Apple's signed confirmation of what was bought and keep a record of it (Apple's number for the payment, what was bought, and when) with your player, so that it is added once, and only to you.

Technical logs

The company that hosts our server records technical information for each request, such as the IP address and the time, for security and to find and fix faults. These logs are kept for a limited time.

What the game does not do

  • It shows no advertising and uses no advertising identifier.
  • It does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • It contains no analytics or third-party trackers.
  • It does not read your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.
  • We do not sell your information, and we do not share it for marketing.

What is kept on your phone

Your settings (music and sounds, the game you last chose), the key to your guest player, and a few small notes such as what you threw most recently. They are removed when you delete the app.

Who handles information for us

  • Supabase hosts our server and database, on servers in the United States: your player, your sign-in, your friends and messages, and the tables you play at.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple and App Store purchases.

They handle this information only to provide those services to us.

How long we keep it

We keep your player until you delete it. Tables you played at may be kept for a time after a game has ended.

Deleting your account

In the game, open your Profile, then Settings, then Delete my account. This removes, at once and for good: your player and everything it holds, your friends, your messages and invitations, your places in the standings, and your sign-in. It cannot be undone. Two things are kept: reports of abuse, and the bare record of each purchase (Apple's number for the payment and what was bought), which carries no name.

If you cannot open the game, write to kuroot.support@gmail.com and we will help.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see the information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, or to object to how it is used. In the game you can change your name and delete everything yourself. For anything else, write to us and we will answer.

Children

Kuroot is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, write to us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
