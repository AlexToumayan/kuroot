Common questions

How do I keep my progress on a new phone?

Open your Profile and choose Sign in with Apple on the phone you play on now. Then sign in the same way on the new phone, and your coins, trophies and everything you own will be there.

How do I play with my friends?

Open Friends and press Create a table, then send the code or the link by WhatsApp or text. Anyone with the code can sit down. You can also add each other with your friend codes, and then invite a friend to your table with one tap.

How do I change my name?

Open your Profile and tap your name.

Where are the rules of each game?

Tap the question mark beside the games in the lobby, or How to play in the menu at a table.

I ran out of time and lost my seat.

Each turn has twenty seconds. If the time runs out, one move is made for you. After three turns in a row with no move from you, a bot takes your seat for the rest of that game. At a friends' table you can sit down again for the next game.

Someone was rude to me.

Open your conversation with them, tap the three dots, and choose Report or Block. Someone you have blocked cannot write to you, invite you or see where you are playing.

How do I turn the music or the sounds off?

Open your Profile, then Settings.

Purchases and refunds

Purchases are made through the App Store. Refunds are handled by Apple: ask for one at reportaproblem.apple.com. If something you bought did not arrive in the game, write to us with your friend code and we will put it right.

How do I delete my account?

Open your Profile, then Settings, then Delete my account. It removes your player, your friends, your messages and your sign-in for good, and it cannot be undone. If you cannot open the game, write to us and we will help.
